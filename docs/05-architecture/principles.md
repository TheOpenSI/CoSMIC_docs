CoSMIC is designed for organisations requiring:

- Data sovereignty
- Local AI deployment
- Secure knowledge management
- Open-source transparency
- Governance and auditability
- Sustainable AI reporting

The modular architecture allows organisations to deploy only the components required for their specific environment.

## Open Science

CoSMIC is developed within the Open Source Institute and aligns with the principles of:

- Open-source software
- FAIR data practices
- Reproducible research
- Transparent AI systems
- Collaborative innovation

This makes CoSMIC particularly suitable for research institutions, government agencies, educational organisations and industry partners seeking trustworthy AI infrastructure.
